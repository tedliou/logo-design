# Case: the nine-grid cat (VerveCode, 2026-11-08)

Read this to see the whole method applied once, and before G4 of any other project to check you are not repeating it. The method transfers; the cat's shapes do not.

## What happened

```
pixel badge from the photo (rejected: thin, meaningless, not a trademark)
  -> measured a geometric reference image, redrew as a 96 then 128 unit seal; whiskers droop as same-radius arcs
  -> brand definition, a style reference, nine-grid ideation; four directions, two rounds of variants
  -> final: a vector mark on a 128 unit grid with one local pixel region
```

The final mark took one day, Opus at max effort, and thirteen interventions by the author. This skill turns those interventions into its structure:

| The author supplied | Kind | Where it lives now |

Four of these decided the direction: the reference and prompt, the brand definition, local pixels, and the cultural association. They are the core of the interview.

## How the final mark works

| Element | Form | Literal | Figurative | Operation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nine cells | 3 x 38 units with 7 unit gutters, 128 canvas | A cat's face in a grid | Structure, modules, code | skeleton |
| Empty top middle cell | Missing cell | Space between the ears | Always one cell left for the next version | figure-ground |
| Left ear | Triangle at 45 degrees | Ear | | silhouette |
| Right ear in pixels | Stepped triangle, two detached pixels | Ear | Games; rendering in progress | cut, motion |
| Slit pupils | Vertical bars in two cells | Cat eyes | Text cursor | substitution |
| V nose | Pentagon in the accent color | Nose | V of Verve; the only warm color is the heart of it | fusion |
| `>_` mouth | Chevron and underscore | Mouth | A terminal prompt: the cat speaks code | substitution |
| Whiskers | Six ribbons, radius 63, taper 8:1, drooping | Whiskers | Sensors: sense first, then act | motion (grounded) |

Its constants are the example in construction.md: grid lines, an angle set, a ribbon curve family and a restricted accent.

## Do not copy

These belong to this mark. When any appears in another project's direction, the reviewer flags it, and E1 (a sunset photo) fails if one does:

- a 3 x 3 grid of square cells with gutters as the face or body;
- a missing cell meaning "the next version";
- a V-shaped accent pentagon as a nose;
- slit-pupil eyes as cursors, a `>_` mouth;
- one ear dissolving into pixels with detached pixels;
- six drooping tapered ribbons;
- the palette near-black #18181b, white #fafafa and crimson #dc3d5c.

What does transfer: measuring before drawing, one accent on the core element, a style signal only where it refers, fusion of the subject with a letter of the name, grounded curves, and a declared grid that a script can check.
